# Opening experiments

Instagram lists skip rate (viewers who leave in the first 3 s) first among the things that
decide a reel's reach. Change one thing per post and compare against the baseline below.

## Baseline (Instagram, read 15 Oct 2026; clips 2 h to 2 d old)

| Clip | First frame | Skip rate | Avg watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chiang Mai | tile + Doi Suthep | 48.7% | 21 s |
| Sukhothai | tile + hills | 50.2% | 24 s |
| Ayutthaya | tile + island outline | 52.6% | 21 s |
| Chachoengsao | flat forest tile | 51.1% | 14 s |
| Chonburi | tile + sea | 47.8% | 15 s |
| Buriram | flat forest tile | 49.2% | 19 s |
| Nakhon Sawan | flat forest tile, rivers | 56.1% | 14 s |
| Phitsanulok | flat forest tile | 52.4% | 25 s |
| Nakhon Phanom | tile + Mekong | 56.5% | 13 s |
| Wat Rai Khing (35 s) | ginger fields | 56.2% | 9 s |

Mean about 52%. Ten clips and an 8-point spread: a hint, not a result.

## 1. Hero on the tile (Korat v2) — read: strong positive

Hypothesis (owner): the first shot can stay the land tile, but something on it has to stand
out. Korat opens closer (camera 320 instead of 430) with three large dinosaurs in warm
ochre/rust against the canopy, visible from frame 0. Everything else as in the other cities.
Read Korat's skip rate after 24 h and compare with the table. Post it on its own, not in a batch.

Result (Instagram, posted 16 Oct, read 17 Oct 09:34, about a day in): **skip rate 32.9%**
(34.2% at 14 h), against a baseline mean of about 52% and a best of 47.8% (Chonburi): 15
points under the best clip, outside the baseline's whole 8-point spread. 41,722 views from
31,598 viewers, far above the typical-reel line. Share 1.3%, like 8.3%, save 0.7%, repost
0.7%, comment 0.1%; 96 follows.

**Average watch 21 s, which is not better than the baseline** (9-25 s; Chiang Mai 21,
Sukhothai 24, Phitsanulok 25). The opening kept far more people past 3 s, but once past it
they left at the usual pace: the hook is solved, the middle of the clip is the next lever.

Retention curve (same read): ~67% at 3 s, **~35% by 17 s**, then nearly flat to ~21% at
59 s. The loss after the hook is in seconds 3-17 (dinosaurs, Ban Prasat, Sema, Phimai:
archaeology, no people); from the town and the armies on (18 s+) people stay. Next lever:
people and motion in the first beat after the title, and a shorter legend.

Caveats: one clip; and the 3-s window also shows the title card, so interest in Korat itself
(a large city, a well-known heroine) is mixed in with the opening. Korat v2 also changed the
story, but skip rate only sees the first 3 s, so the opening is the likeliest cause.

Next: keep a hero on the tile at frame 0 for the next city (Nakhon Si: the whole tile, then
down to the relic boat by 3.6 s; a replication, not a new variable), and run experiment 2
only after that one is read. Read average watch time and the retention curve on every post
from now on: that is where the next gain is.

## 1b. Replication: Nakhon Si (posted 17 Oct 2026) — read: partial

Same opening rule as Korat: the whole tile under the title, then down to the relic boat by
3.6 s. The boat is smaller on the tile at frame 0 than Korat's dinosaurs, so this also tests
whether the wide establishing shot alone carries the hook. 75 s, not 60, so compare skip
rate (first 3 s) directly, and average watch as a share of the length. Also read the
retention curve: the legend beat now has the boat moving from frame 0.

Result (read 18 Oct 08:59, about a day in): **skip rate 43.1%**. Better than the old baseline
(mean ~52%, best 47.8%) but **10 points worse than Korat's 32.9%**. 9,417 views from 7,000
viewers (Korat: 41,722), 30 follows, share 0.8%, like 6.6%, save 0.6%. Average watch 21 s of
75 (28%; Korat 21 of 60). Retention ~57% at 3 s, ~31% by 17 s, ~22% at 54 s, then a slide
to ~12% over the last 10 s.

Reading: the wide tile under the title helps a little, but it is the **hero** that did most of
Korat's work -- the relic boat is a speck at frame 0 (the cover frame shows forest and a dot),
the dinosaurs filled it. After the hook the two clips behave alike: both keep a bit over half
of the 3-s audience to 17 s, then stay nearly flat. The 75 s tail loses people in the finale;
a 75 s clip needs its finale to earn the extra seconds. Caveat: one clip each, and Korat's
audience (a big city, a famous heroine) is larger to begin with.

Rule from here: **a hero big enough to read at frame 0 on the whole tile**, sized for the
whole-tile shot (check_story's legibility rule with an honest `size`), not for the close-up.
Lampang's 11x carriage is the next test of it.

## 1c. Lampang: a hero from the city's present on the old tile — read: did not replicate

Owner's call: keep the usual forest tile and put a Lampang horse carriage on it at hero scale
from frame 0 (no rewind). Unlike the dinosaurs and the relic boat, the hero is not from the
opening era; it is the city's best-known symbol, and it returns in its own beat (1916). Read
skip rate and the retention curve as for 1 and 1b.

Result (read 19 Oct 09:42, a little over a day in): **skip rate 44.9%**: no better than Nakhon
Si's 43.1%, and 12 points worse than Korat's 32.9%. 17,179 views from 13,388 viewers, the
second-largest reach after Korat, well above the typical-reel line. Share 1.2%, like 8.0%,
save 0.7%, repost 0.9%, comment 0.2%; 67 follows. Average watch **18 s of 60**, the lowest of
the three (Korat 21, Nakhon Si 21 of 75). Views: Reels tab 76.9%, Explore 9.1%, Feed 7.4%,
Stories 3.0%, Profile 2.7%. Retention, read off the curve, so approximate: ~60% at 3 s,
~30% by 17 s, then a slow slide to ~20% at 55 s and a drop at the very end.

Reading: **a big hero alone did not repeat Korat's hook.** The carriage reads clearly at frame
0 (it fills the cover frame as the dinosaurs did), yet the skip rate landed with Nakhon Si's
speck of a boat. What the dinosaurs had that the carriage lacks:
- surprise: a prehistoric animal on a city tile;
- motion from frame 0: they walk; the carriage stands until its own beat;
- a link to the title: Korat ↔ dinosaurs is a known local fact, whereas a carriage on a forest
  is a symbol out of its era.

Engagement held up (share and like rates match Korat's), so people who stay like the clip;
the leak is still the first 3 s and seconds 3-17.

Caveats: three clips, each with a different audience and story. Korat's dinosaurs may still be
a one-off.

Next (Phetchaburi): the planned frame-0 hero is Khao Wang, a static hill, which this result
says is not enough. Give the opening **something moving and surprising** under the title, not
just something big: e.g. the palace building itself up on the peaks, or the festival fireworks
over the hill as a 1-s flash-forward (which is experiment 2's question, so pick one and say
which).

## 1d. Khon Kaen: the landmark at hero scale, a dinosaur at the tile's edge — read: no better

Cover: the whole tile with พระมหาธาตุแก่นนคร at hero scale in the middle and a small dinosaur at
the right edge (the fossils of Phu Wiang). Built before the storyline standard: the stupa stands
still, and the dinosaur is small and off-centre.

Result (read 20 Oct 08:55, just under a day in): **skip rate 44.7%**. Lampang was 44.9% and Nakhon
Si 43.1%; Korat's 32.9% is still the only clear win. 12,117 views from 8,848 viewers, 26
follows. Average watch **19 s of 60**. Share **1.4%**, the highest of the four, with likes at
6.8%, saves at 0.5%, reposts at 0.7% and comments at 0.3% (above the typical reel). The
counts were 638 likes, 25 comments, 65 reposts, 126 shares and 44 saves. Views came from the
Reels tab (75.5%), Feed (9.2%), Explore (9.1%), Profile (3.0%) and Stories (2.1%).
Retention, read off the curve and so approximate: ~59% at 3 s, ~41% at 8 s, **~34% by 17 s**,
then a slow slide to ~19% at 55 s and a drop at the end.

Reading:
- **The hook result repeats for the third time.** A big but static landmark or symbol on the
  tile lands at 43–45% skip, whatever it is (relic boat, carriage, stupa). The dinosaur that
  worked for Korat did not work as a small figure at the tile's edge: Korat's three filled the
  frame and walked. This is the case for the standard's hero rules: **big, centred, and
  moving**, not merely present.
- **Retention at 17 s (~34%)** is at the top of the 30–35% band, level with Korat. Khon Kaen's
  first beats already had moving dinosaurs and a caravan. That is weak support for "motion in
  3–17 s", not proof.
- **Shares are the best so far (1.4%).** They follow the province's own symbol (finding 6), and
  Khon Kaen's dinosaur story is one locals pass on.

Next: Phetchaburi (experiment 3) is the first clip with a hero that is centred, surprising
(a dessert as big as the hill) and moving in the first second. If its skip rate falls toward
Korat's while 17-s retention holds, the hero rules are confirmed.

## 3. Storyline standard v1 (Phetchaburi) — read: worse on every measure

This is not one variable. Phetchaburi is the first clip built to the whole of
`docs/storyline.md`, which draws on 1–1c and the owner's notes:

- **Hook:** a hero with all four marks of the scorecard. Korat's dinosaurs had all four;
  Lampang's carriage had two.
  - On screen: Khao Wang with a hill-sized tray of ขนมหม้อแกง beside it, a piece lifting out
    while the camera pushes in.
  - Its own sound from 0.2 s: a server going under the custard.
- **3–17 s:**
  - porters and builders from 3.4 s;
  - from 9 s, the siege: a fleet and two armies.

  Something moves on screen for at least 70% of the window, which is checked. In Korat this
  stretch was archaeology with no people in it.
- **Open loop:** the tray returns at the fair at 45 s, with the same sound.
- **Ending:** the counter lands on พ.ศ. 2569 and holds before the finale card, which has a
  tagline. The clip is 60 s.

Read about 24 h after posting:

- **Skip rate**, against Korat's 32.9% and Lampang's 44.9%. Does a moving, surprising hero work
  without dinosaurs?
- **Retention at 17 s**, against 30–35% on every clip so far. This is the number the standard
  is meant to move.
- **Average watch**, against 18–21 s.
- **Shares**, against 0.8–1.3%. ขนมหม้อแกง is the province's best-known product, so finding 6
  predicts a share rate at Korat's level or above.

Caveat: the clip tests a package, so a gain cannot be pinned on one rule.
- If retention at 17 s moves and skip rate does not, the middle worked and the hook did not.
- If skip rate moves and retention at 17 s does not, it is the other way round.

From the next city on, change one thing at a time against this standard.

### Result (read 21 Oct 08:54, just under a day in)

**Skip rate 50.4%**, the worst of the five reads and back at the forest-tile baseline (~52%).
5,632 views from 3,984 viewers, under half of a typical reel at the same age (the views curve
sits at ~6.5K against ~13K for the typical reel), 12 follows. **Average watch 15 s of 60.**
**Share 0.7%**, the lowest after Nakhon Si's 0.8%; likes 5.9%, saves 0.5%, reposts 0.7% (all
"lower"); comments 0.3%, the only rate Instagram marks "higher". Counts: 249 likes, 12 comments,
28 reposts, 29 shares, 21 saves. Views came from the Reels tab (62.3%), Feed (19.8%), Stories
(6.8%), Explore (6.5%) and Profile (3.4%).

Retention, read off the curve and so approximate: **~51% at 3 s**, ~38% at 7 s, **~24% at 17 s**,
~20% at 27 s, ~9% at 55 s.

| | predicted (above) | read |
|---|---|---|
| skip rate | toward Korat's 32.9% | 50.4% |
| retention at 17 s | above 30–35% | ~24% |
| average watch | above 18–21 s | 15 s |
| shares | Korat's 1.3% or above | 0.7% |

Reading:
- **Both halves failed, so the caveat above cannot split them.** By its own rule, neither the
  hook nor the middle worked; the package as a whole did worse than the clips built before it.
- **The hero passed the check but not the eye.** `storyline.mjs` confirms a `hero` actor is
  centred under the title for 0–2 s; it cannot tell whether the thing reads at phone size. On
  the cover, Khao Wang is a green lump with grey halls, and the tray of ขนมหม้อแกง, the surprise,
  is a small brown box at the corner; the piece that lifts out is a few pixels. In practice
  it is the fourth static landmark or symbol on the tile (relic boat, carriage, stupa, hill and
  tray), and those have landed at 43–50% every time. Korat's walking dinosaurs, three of them
  filling the frame, remain the only clear win.
- **The middle had motion and still lost people faster.** Porters from 3.4 s and the siege from
  9 s kept actors on screen through 3–17 s, yet 17-s retention fell from Khon Kaen's ~34% to
  ~24%. Motion that a viewer has to search for (small figures on a wide tile) does not hold
  them; this is the same lesson as the hero, one beat later.
- **Shares did not follow the province's symbol** (finding 6 predicted ≥ 1.3%). ขนมหม้อแกง was on
  screen at both ends and shares were 0.7%. Finding 6 holds for Korat, Lampang and Khon Kaen and
  fails here; it is weaker than it looked.
- **Things this read cannot rule out:** the topic's own pull (a smaller audience for
  Phetchaburi than for Korat or Khon Kaen), the posting time, and the lower reach itself, which
  follows the skip rate rather than causing it.

What it changes:
- A hero has to be **big on the phone screen and visibly moving in the first second**, like
  Korat's: large living things crossing the tile. A landmark with a small moving part is a
  static landmark. The legibility rule (`scale × size ≥ 80 px`) was set for actors in a beat
  and is too lax for a hero; measure Korat's dinosaurs at 0–2 s before setting a hero floor.
- Chiang Rai's hero (วัดร่องขุ่น at hero scale, hands rising) is in the same static class, so
  this read predicts a skip rate in the 43–50% band for it as built.
