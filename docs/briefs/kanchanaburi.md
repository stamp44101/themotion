## Why Kanchanaburi next (owner said "ลุย", 3 Nov 2026, from the shortlist)

- **Hero and big event in one scene:** the Kwai bridge with a train on it. Matches the Ratchaburi/Saraburi
  lesson (the first beat after the cover must be big and close, and the symbol must move).
- **Rail plus river:** the bridge over the Khwae Yai is a rule the engine already has (a railway bridges
  every river it crosses). The river is a curved centreline (`path`), not stacked bands.
- **A real walled town:** Rama III moved the seat from Lat Ya to Pak Phraek in 2374 and built the wall
  and gates (a footprint to trace from ONEP, so `city.outline` is not a rectangle).
- **Payoff that is alive today:** the Kwai bridge week (light-and-sound, fireworks, the train run) each
  late Nov–early Dec: 27 Nov–7 Dec 2568 last year; this year's dates are for research.

## Sensitivity (decide before the storyline)

The Death Railway means 1942–45 prisoners and Asian labourers who died building it. Rule for this clip:
**no depicted suffering, no bodies, no guards**; the war reads as the railway being driven through the
forest by many tiny figures, the bombing as the bridge breaking and the Thai government mending it (2489),
and a memorial beat (the Chungkai/Don Rak cemeteries, a still moment, no music sting). The owner decides
how much of the war the clip carries at all; the safe option is the bridge's own life (built, broken,
mended, a festival) with the war as one beat.
