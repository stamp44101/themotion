# Udon Thani (อุดรธานี) — brief (v1)

Research: `udonthani_research_request.md` → `udonthani_research_gemini.md` (Gemini via Antigravity CLI,
28 Oct 2026). Map: `udonthani_onep/` ([local]: no ONEP old-town polygon; OSM lake, hall, pillar, monument,
station and line). Look: shader only. Length: 60 s.

Chosen for the **0-to-100 spine** (owner, 27 Oct: three flat posts; storyline v2): the tile is forest and a
lake when the 1893 column arrives, and the town fills it beat by beat until the whole tile is city.

## §4 Hero — decided (owner, 28 Oct 2026): **A1, the Ban Chiang jar**

Style frames `udonthani_styleframes_A1.jpg` / `_A2.jpg`. A giant jar is already rising out of its dig at
frame 0 and an unseen brush whips the red scrolls round it in 1.6 s; a red ring runs out over the ground.
Owner's pick A1, with my two changes: the jar ~20% bigger (HERO_S 9.6, ~31 units) and pale terracotta
instead of cream. It shrinks to a marker at Ban Chiang (drawn on the tile's east side; the site is 48 km
due east) and comes back as a giant jar lantern on the Thung Si Mueang fair ground (the lantern is our
image of the fair, not a claim that one stands there: the caption does not mention it).
