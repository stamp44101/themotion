Status: research pass 1 (20 ตุลาคม 2569), written in the cloud container with web search only.

## 0. What the last clips say (docs/experiments.md)

- **A hero that is static fails, whatever it is.** Nakhon Si's boat, Lampang's carriage and Khon
  Kaen's stupa all landed at 43–45% skip. Only Korat's big walking dinosaurs won (32.9%).
- **Khon Kaen shared best (1.4%)** with a symbol locals are proud of and pass on.
- Retention at 17 s was best (~34–35%) where the first beats had animals or people moving.
- **60 s.**

## 3. Story (proposal, 60 s, storyline standard)

### 3a. Storyline worksheet

Two hero options; the owner picks (§7.1).

```
Hero A (0-3 s): วัดร่องขุ่น at hero scale on the tile, glittering white and mirror-silver, the
               "hands of the cycle of rebirth" reaching up out of the ground in front of it
               BIG yes   IDENTITY yes (the province's best-known image)
               MOVING yes (the hands rise, mirror glints sweep)   SURPRISING yes (a white
               temple bigger than the town, on a 13th-century forest tile)
Hero B (0-3 s): พญามังราย's runaway war elephant, giant, walking across the tile toward ดอยจอมทอง
               BIG yes   IDENTITY yes (the founding legend every local school child knows)
               MOVING yes (walks, swings its trunk)   SURPRISING yes (out of scale)
               -- the nearest thing to Korat's walking dinosaurs, the only hook that worked
Hero sound: A: a temple bell and a glassy shimmer; B: a footfall and a trumpet
Open loop: A -> the temple at the payoff (1997 -> today, City of Design 2566)
           B -> answered at 3-8 s (it leads Mangrai to the hill); its callback at the payoff
                is [open: needs an honest one, e.g. elephants in a real present-day procession]
First event (3-9 s, people): Mangrai's men following the elephant; the เวียง laid out round
           ดอยจอมทอง by the Kok (พ.ศ. 1805)
Spectacle (by 12 s): a storm; lightning splits the chedi at วัดป่าเยียะ and the Emerald Buddha
           is found (พ.ศ. 1977); the elephant procession that will not go to Chiang Mai
Middle beats, one verb each: fall/abandonment (Burmese era, the town left to forest) /
           arrive (re-founded พ.ศ. 2386, people come back by raft and cart) / grow
Grow shot: the modern town spreading south from the Kok, airport, the clock tower
Payoff (45-54 s, people + hero back): วัดร่องขุ่น and the City of Design (31 ต.ค. 2566),
           crowds at the temple
Tagline: [from the provincial slogan: "เหนือสุดในสยาม ... " — exact wording to check]
Facts with nothing to show (sub lines only): the move of the capital from เงินยาง,
           Thailand Biennale 2566, Tham Luang (60 km away, and a rescue with a death in it:
           not a beat)
```
