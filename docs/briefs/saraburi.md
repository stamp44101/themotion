## Why Saraburi next

- **A link to the last clip.** The Ratchaburi resident said the Thai Yuan live in two provinces,
  Saraburi and Ratchaburi, both moved down from Chiang Saen. Saraburi is the other half of that
  journey (Sao Hai, the Thai Yuan villages on the Pa Sak); a sub line can name the link.
- **A legend with an animal in it.** The Buddha's Footprint (พระพุทธบาท) found in the reign of
  King Songtham by a hunter (พรานบุญ) following a wounded deer that drank from the print and was
  healed: the strongest hook candidate so far (big, moving, the city's own story).
- **A town that moved.** The seat was at Sao Hai and later moved to Pak Phriao on the Pa Sak, the
  town of today (which years: research).
- **0 to 100:** a small Ayutthaya-era river town, the northeastern railway, the cement works in the
  limestone hills (Siam Cement at Tha Luang / Kaeng Khoi), the Friendship Highway to Korat, and an
  industrial town at the gate to the Isan plateau.

## First dates checked on the web (1 Nov 2026)

- **Founding:** "สันนิษฐานว่าตั้งขึ้นประมาณ พ.ศ. 2092" in the reign of Maha Chakkraphat
  (กศน.อำเภอเมืองสระบุรี; สำนักงานธนารักษ์พื้นที่สระบุรี). The province's own page
  (saraburi.go.th, ประวัติความเป็นมา) says only that the town existed before **2112** (named in the
  chronicle under Mahinthrathirat) and that no document gives its founding: **2092 is a
  supposition**; a caption may say "ราว 2092" at most, or use 2112.
- **The seat moved from Sao Hai to Pak Phriao** (library.tru.ac.th, สระบุรี ประวัติ-สภาพทั่วไป):
  the year is for the research.

## Story we expect (to be checked, nothing here is on screen yet)

From memory, every year unverified unless listed above:
- Pre-Ayutthaya: Dvaravati / Lopburi-era finds in the Pa Sak valley? (research)
- ~2092 / 2112: the town of Saraburi, first at or near Sao Hai on the Pa Sak.
- ~2150s–2160s (King Songtham): the Footprint found on Khao Suwan Banphot; the mondop, the royal
  road and processions from Ayutthaya; the fair of the 3rd and 4th months.
- 2347 (Rama I): Chiang Saen taken; its people brought south, settled at Sao Hai (and Ratchaburi).
- Rama V: the town moves to Pak Phriao; the northeastern railway passes (Kaeng Khoi ~2441,
  Korat 2443?).
- 2456: Siam Cement's works at Bang Sue get limestone from Saraburi / a plant at Tha Luang (check).
- 2501: the Friendship Highway (ถนนมิตรภาพ) from Saraburi to Korat.
- Today: ตักบาตรดอกไม้ (flower alms, ดอกเข้าพรรษา) at Wat Phra Phutthabat, the cement town, Khao
  Sam Lan, the Thai Yuan of Sao Hai.

Far places (storyline v2 rule 3, at most two): Wat Phra Phutthabat is ~25–30 km north of Mueang
Saraburi; Kaeng Khoi / Tha Luang ~15–20 km east. Research must say which beats belong to the town
and which to the far places, so the tile can hold the town and one far place, or the tile is the
Pa Sak corridor.

## Hero options (owner decides after research and style frames)

- **A. The deer and the Footprint.** A giant wounded deer runs out of the forest at frame 0,
  drinks from a huge footprint in the rock, and is healed (light, rising); the hunter follows.
  Payoff: the flower-alms procession to the same footprint. BIG, IDENTITY, MOVING, SURPRISING,
  the city's own legend.
- **B. Flower alms (ตักบาตรดอกไม้).** A legend-scale yellow ดอกเข้าพรรษา opening over the tile,
  monks walking out from under it. Moving and shareable, but it is the ending, not a start.
- **C. The Thai Yuan journey from Chiang Saen** (a callback to Ratchaburi). Small figures; better
  as the first event (3–9 s) than as the cover.
- **D.** Whatever the research ranks higher (the "weeping pillar" story behind the name Sao Hai,
  if it is one; Khao Sam Lan; the cement hills).
