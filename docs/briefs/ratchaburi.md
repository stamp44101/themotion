## Local input (owner's chat with a Ratchaburi resident, 30 Oct 2026)

The first city whose storyline starts from someone who lives there. The owner asked a resident of
Mueang Ratchaburi (a follower; name kept out of the repo) where locals send visitors; the owner will not ask
further, so everything below is final input and the rest comes from research.

- **Visitors go to Suan Phueng** (resorts, Alpaca Hill). Far from the town (~50 km west): a sub line
  at most, not a beat (storyline v2 rule 3, at most two far places).
- **The old town has an old market, ตลาดเก่าโคยกี๊ (Koi Ki)**, a walking street in the old shophouses
  by the Mae Klong. A grow-shot / today place.
- **Ratchaburi people are a mix:** ญวน (Vietnamese), จีน (Chinese), and the resident's own roots,
  ไทยวน, ไทลื้อ, ไทพวน and ไทครั่ง (ลาวครั่ง), living together.
- **Thai Yuan live in two provinces, Saraburi and Ratchaburi, moved down from the north.** The
  resident's message "เราอพยพมาจาก เมืองเ..." was half-covered by a sticker; almost certainly
  **เมืองเชียงแสน** (to verify by research, not by asking).
- **Famous for ผ้าซิ่นตีนจก** (the Thai Yuan's tin-chok skirt; Khu Bua is the known centre).
- **"คนราชบุรีชอบปั้นโอ่งขาย — โอ่งดินเผามังกร"**: the dragon jar, the thing the resident named
  unprompted as what Ratchaburi people make.

What it gives the clip: an identity the locals themselves name (the dragon jar), a people's journey
(Chiang Saen to the Mae Klong), a craft to end on (tin-chok weaving), and a town of many peoples
round one river.

## Story we expect (to be checked, nothing here is on screen yet)

From memory, every year unverified:
- Khu Bua, a Dvaravati town south of today's city; Wat Mahathat Ratchaburi's prang on the Mae Klong.
- Thai Yuan brought from Chiang Saen in the reign of Rama I (~2347) and settled at Khu Bua.
- A walled town on the Mae Klong in the early Bangkok era (which bank, which years: research).
- The southern railway and its Mae Klong bridge (~2444–2446); the bridge bombed in WWII.
- Chinese potters start the dragon-jar kilns (~2476).
- The Damnoen Saduak canal (Rama IV) — a far place, sub line or out.

## Hero options (owner decides after research and style frames)

- **A. The dragon jar.** A giant jar thrown on a wheel on the riverbank in the first second, a dragon
  coiling up round it as the glaze fires; the payoff brings the jars back stacked by the river and on
  boats. BIG, IDENTITY, MOVING, SURPRISING. The local's own pick.
- **B. The journey.** A Thai Yuan column with ox carts and looms walking down from the north into
  forest by the Mae Klong; the payoff is the tin-chok looms. Moving and true, but small figures.
- **C.** Whatever the research ranks higher.

## Map ([local], commit 0551c63, `ratchaburi_onep/`)

- **ONEP: an announced old town** (เมืองเก่าราชบุรี, OT_CODE 24): zone 1 is 1.56 km², ~1,770 × 1,450 m,
  and takes in the Mae Klong bend; Wat Mahathat in its north-west, the national museum south of the bend.
- **Mae Klong ~143 m wide through town** (110–208 m) → ~1.8 units at 80 m/unit.
- Within ~2 km of the bend: Wat Mahathat, Tao Hong Tai (1.1 km W), Chedi Hak (1.8 km W), the Southern
  Line rail bridge (13.5414, 99.8242), the Phetkasem road bridge, the station (2.2 km SE).
- Far places (rule 3, at most two): Khao Ngu 4.8 km NW, Khu Bua 6–7 km S.
- Not in OSM: the old wall/moat, Koi Ki market, older kilns, the Thai Yuan village as such.
