## Cover: A1, picked by the owner (12 Nov)
`samutsongkhram_styleframes.jpg`, code `src/styleframes/samutsongkhram.tsx` (`SskStyleA1`). A round steel steaming tray
(เต๊า) with 13 bamboo baskets (เข่ง); at frame 0 half hold their three pla thu, heads bent down (หน้างอคอหัก); the rest are
packed one basket at a time, and at ~2.3 s the tray steams. B (the railway market's awnings folding for the train) is kept
for the payoff.

## Map in one paragraph
The Mae Klong runs N→S down the west edge of the town (170-250 m wide), loops west of it, and meets the gulf ~4.9 km S;
Don Hoi Lot is the mud flat east of the mouth. The Mae Klong railway comes in from the NE through coconut orchards and
ends at the station ~100 m from the bank; its last ~300 m is the railway market. Wat Phet Samut is on the bank just N of
the station, the fort site (now Somdet Phra Phutthaloetla hospital) ~150 m S. Amphawa is ~5.3 km W (tile edge), Wat Bang
Kung 7.7 km NW (off the tile). Orchard ditches everywhere; salt pans and shrimp ponds to the SE.
