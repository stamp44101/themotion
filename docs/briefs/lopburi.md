## 4. Hero (to choose from style frames)

Every option is something large and moving on the whole tile from frame 0 that returns in the payoff.

- **A. Monkeys at hero scale.** A troop of macaques, drawn large (the way Korat's dinosaurs
  are), leaping across พระปรางค์สามยอด. Callback: the 2532 buffet. Instantly Lopburi; Gemini's
  warning is that true-scale monkeys read as ants, so they must be oversized and close.
- **B. Wild elephants splashing through ทะเลชุบศร.** Large and moving; ties to the 1155
  elephants for China and Narai's elephant hunts. Callback: the elephants lining the French
  embassy's route, or the festival procession. Less instantly "Lopburi" than monkeys.
- **C. Hanuman.** The city legend: Rama's arrow lands, the white earth (ดินสอพอง) and
  ทะเลชุบศร form, and the city is given to Hanuman; a giant white monkey leaps across the tile,
  as Ubon's naga uncoils. Callback: his "descendants" at the buffet. Legend, so marked as such.

## 6. Cautions (from the research, §5)

- Narai died on **11 July** 2231, not 18 July.
- พระปรางค์สามยอด is a Mahayana Buddhist sanctuary of Jayavarman VII, not a Hindu shrine and not
  built by Narai (he added the vihara in front). ปรางค์แขก is the Hindu one.
- The palace was **not** burned by the Burmese in 2310; it decayed after 2231.
- Monkeys: show them with affection (ศิษย์เจ้าพ่อพระกาฬ, the buffet). If 2567 is mentioned at all:
  "จัดระเบียบบ้านใหม่ให้ลิง" — never "a city ruined by monkeys".
- Army camps: say "ศูนย์การทหารปืนใหญ่" / "เมืองทหารยุคจอมพล ป.", not the renamed camp names.
