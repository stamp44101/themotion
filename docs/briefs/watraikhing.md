# Landmark brief — Wat Rai Khing / วัดไร่ขิง (วัดมงคลจินดาราม)

Sam Phran, Nakhon Pathom, on the south bank of the Tha Chin (Nakhon Chai Si) River.
35-second landmark reel at 2 m per world unit (`src/landmarks/watraikhing/`).

## Facts used, with confidence

| fact | used as | confidence | sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name from the ginger fields (ไร่ขิง) of Chinese growers, also the tambon name | caption 1 | documented (consistent across sources) | Dailynews, Thairath, nkppao.go.th |
| Founded by Somdet Phra Phutthachan (Phuk), then Phra Thammaratchanuwat, abbot of Wat Sala Pun, on his home ground | caption 2 | documented | Dailynews, Silpa-Mag, TrueID |
| Founding year **พ.ศ. 2394 (1851)** | caption 2, "สันนิษฐานว่า" | **disputed**: Thairath and nkppao.go.th give พ.ศ. 2334 (1791). Most sources tie the founding to Rama IV's reign, which fits 2394; no primary record found | Dailynews, Silpa-Mag, TrueID vs Thairath, nkppao |
| Luang Pho Wat Rai Khing: bronze, Mara-vijaya, Chiang Saen style, brought from Wat Sala Pun | caption 3, raft | image and origin documented; **the raft journey (bamboo raft down the Chao Phraya into the Tha Chin) is legend**, and no year is recorded, so the caption says "ตำนานเล่าว่า" and names no year | mgronline, Silpa-Mag, dhammathai |
| Renamed วัดมงคลจินดาราม by Somdet Phra Maha Samana Chao Krom Phraya Vajirananavarorasa, **พ.ศ. 2446 (1903)** | caption 4 | documented ("ราว 2446" in some) | dhammathai, Thairath |
| Annual Luang Pho gilding fair, mid fifth lunar month (around Songkran), nine days and nights | caption 5, finale | documented | Sanook, Kapook, Thairath 2026 guides |
| Royal temple, third class, 10 July 2533 (1990) | not shown (no visible change) | most sources say 2533; one says 2531 | dhammathai, TrueID |

Left out on purpose: the 2025 temple finance scandal (Thai PBS World). It is not part of
the temple's history arc and is still before the courts.

## Representative, not surveyed

Network policy blocked image sites from the build container, so these follow a generic
early-Rattanakosin ubosot and Thai temple-fair practice, not photographs of this temple:
- ubosot proportions (40 x 18 m), two-tier telescoping roof, gilded pediments, eight sema
- the compound plan: kamphaeng kaeo, bell tower, two chedis, sala kan parian
- years for the bell tower, chedis (from founding + 4-8 years) and sala (1895-1900)
- the covered fish-feeding sala at the landing (from 1990)
- the Ferris wheel and stall layout at the fair
