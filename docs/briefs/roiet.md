## Cover: A2 โหวด, picked by the owner (15 Nov, "ทำตามเขา": the cloud's recommendation)
`roiet_styleframes.jpg` (0/1/2/3 s), code `src/styleframes/roiet.tsx` (`RoietStyleA1`, `A2`, `B`). All three stand on the
whole land tile at legend scale in พ.ศ. 2318 (the founding year): the real moat ring (OSM centreline) with a broken earth
bank, บึงพลาญชัย with its island, ดงกุ่ม forest inside the moat, paddy outside. Half made by hand at frame 0, changing shape.
- **A1 แคน.** A giant แคนแปด (two rafts of 8 bamboo pipes through a ประดู่ windchest) lying on the land; half its pipes in
  at frame 0, the rest slide in, rattan bands tie them, then it lifts and its pipes light pair by pair as it plays. The town's
  own craft (อ.เมือง slogan "แหล่งผลิตแคนลือเลื่อง", Ban Si Kaeo 15 km NW).
- **A2 โหวด (cloud's pick).** A giant wot standing upright: half its ring of bamboo pipes round the hub at frame 0, the rest
  drop in tallest to shortest (a spiral), the black wax seals the foot, then it spins and rings of sound roll out over the
  land. The province's own instrument and the shape of **หอโหวด ๑๐๑**, which can rise on the bueng's south bank at the end:
  the callback is built into the town.
- **B ข้าวหอมมะลิ.** A giant sheaf: half its stalks bound at frame 0, the rest swing in, the straw band ties, the ears
  ripen gold and droop. On the provincial seal, but Thung Kula is ~50 km S, off the tile, and five provinces share it.
- Why A2: it reads as one bold shape at thumbnail size (a round tower of bamboo), it grows visibly, and the same shape comes
  back as the tallest thing in town at the payoff. The khaen is the strongest sound but is shared with all Isan and Laos
  (Kalasin's cover was a loom and a pong lang; Roi Et gets bamboo-gold and black wax, not crimson).

## Map in one paragraph
The town is a rounded-square Dvaravati moat ~1.75 km across, still water except the north half of its west side, with
บึงพลาญชัย (~450 m, an island in two lobes) in the middle; the city pillar is on the island, หอโหวด ๑๐๑ on the south bank.
Roads radiate from the moat to a ring road ~8.5-9 km across (off the tile). No river: แม่น้ำชี is 13 km E. An old airstrip lies 1.5 km NNW
(the first airfield, 2465 per a snippet); the airport (2542) is 15 km ENE; หนองหญ้าม้า reservoir 3–5 km S. No railway yet.
Far places: Ban Si Kaeo (khaen) 15 km NW, the airport 15 km ENE, พระมหาเจดีย์ชัยมงคล 78 km ENE.

  from 1700 (the founders come in over it), the modern roads' crossings are bridges from the 1930s-40s.
