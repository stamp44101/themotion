## Cover: style frames for the owner to pick (15 Nov)
`phayao_styleframes.jpg` (0/1/2/3 s), code `src/styleframes/phayao.tsx` (`PhayaoStyleA1`, `A2`, `B`). All three sit on the
whole land tile (the real Kwan, the Ing, the wiang outline) at legend scale; A1 and A2 are half made by hand at frame 0 and
change shape (experiments §17-§20).
- **A1 พระเจ้าตนหลวง (cloud's pick).** The giant seated Buddha of Wat Si Khom Kham, on its real site on the shore. At frame 0
  the pedestal and the lap are stuccoed white and the chest is raw brick in a bamboo scaffold with people carrying bricks; the
  courses climb to the shoulders and the head rises (ushnisha and flame finial) by 1.7 s, the stucco sweeps up after it by
  2.5 s, the scaffold comes down and the gold goes on from the lap up by 3.4 s. The province's seal and slogan; ~36% of the
  frame's height at 0 s, ~42% at 3 s; its finial stays under the subtitle.
- **A2 วัดติโลกอาราม.** A Lanna bell chedi half laid in brick on the Ing's bank at frame 0; the bell and spire go up and are
  stuccoed by 2 s; then the lake spreads and the chedi sinks to its bell (the temple drowned by the 2482 gate). Surprising,
  but the temple is ~250 m off the south-east shore, so from the cover's side land shows round it and the "sunk in a lake"
  read is weaker; bell chedi covers are common.
- **B ปลาบึก.** A giant Mekong catfish (the province's aquatic animal, bred at the Kwan's fisheries station) leaps across the
  lake from frame 0. A creature, moving, but not made by hand and does not change shape (the §19-§20 rule), and the fish
  belongs to the Mekong more than to Phayao.
- Why A1: a symbol the province owns (seal + slogan + the yearly Paet Peng), half made by hand you can feel (bricks, scaffold,
  people), and a shape change readable at thumbnail size (a headless brick figure becomes a golden Buddha). A2's drowning is
  kept for the 2482 beat in the middle.

## Map in one paragraph
Wiang Phayao is a headland (~1.0 × 1.4 km) in the south-east corner of the Kwan, moat traces on its north and east sides,
lake shore west and south; the city pillar is in it. Wat Si Khom Kham is on the shore 1.7 km NW. The Ing enters the lake at
its north end and leaves at the south-east corner 1.4 km ESE of the pillar, under ถนนพหลโยธิน: the site of the 2482 gate.
Wat Tilok Aram is in the lake 0.9 km S, ~250 m off the headland. The lake is ~6.5 × 6 km with the Phi Pan Nam range behind
its west shore. The town grew north and east along ถนนพหลโยธิน. Far places: Doi Bussarakham (Wat Analayo) 9.5 km W, the
University of Phayao 15 km S.
