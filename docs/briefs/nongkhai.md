## Cover: A1, cloud's pick (delegated)
`nongkhai_styleframes.jpg` (0/1/2/3 s), code `src/styleframes/nongkhai.tsx` (`NongKhaiStyleA1`, `A2`). Both sit on the whole
land tile at legend scale with the Mekong and Laos behind, half made by hand at frame 0 and changing shape (experiments
§17-§20).
- **A1 เรือยาวหัวพญานาค (pick).** A giant long boat dug from one log lies on the bank; at frame 0 its stern half is hollowed
  and painted (dark hull, gold bands, the naga's tail curling up), its bow half still a round log in bark. The adze front runs
  to the bow (chips fly) by 1.7 s and the naga's head comes out of the rough block, gold and green with red eyes. It is the
  first governor's boat Kham Yat (dug ~2370, 40 paddlers) and today's Ok Phansa races on the Mekong: the payoff answers it.
- **A2 พญานาคปูนปั้น.** A giant seven-headed naga in concrete like Sala Kaew Ku's; at frame 0 the coils are cast, three heads
  stand on bare rebar; the other heads are cast one by one and the hood fans out. Strong, but Ubon already opened on a naga,
  Sala Kaew Ku's year is disputed, and it has no people to come back with.
- Why A1: the shape change is the biggest (a log turns into a naga boat), it reads at thumbnail size, and it ties the founding
  to today's boat race, the town's own festival.

## Map in one paragraph
The town is a 3 km strip on the Mekong's south bank; the river runs SW to NE, 550-650 m wide, Laos (Tha Deua) on the far
bank. From the origin (อนุสาวรีย์ปราบฮ่อ): Tha Sadet market (Ban Phai) 1 km NE on the bank, Wat Pho Chai 1.9 km ENE, the
fallen stupa 3.6 km NE in the river, the old station on the bank 1.3 km W, the Friendship Bridge 2.7 km W, Sala Kaew Ku
4.5 km E. Everything is at true scale on one 8.2 km tile; no far place is drawn closer.
