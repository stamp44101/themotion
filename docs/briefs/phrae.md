## Cover: A1 (cloud's pick, delegated)
`phrae_styleframes.jpg` (0/1/2/3 s), code `src/styleframes/phrae.tsx` (`PhraeStyleA1`, `A2`). Both on the whole land tile at
legend scale, half made by hand at frame 0 and changing shape (experiments §17-§20).
- **A1 ลายขนมปังขิง (picked).** A giant teak gable on two trestles. At frame 0 its left half is already fret-sawn into gingerbread
  lace (a sunburst of wedges, rounds along the slopes, scallops along the valance), the right half is plain teak with the pattern
  pencilled on; a bow saw cuts the rest hole by hole until 2.1 s, each cut-out dropping out; then the lace gable lifts and a
  pink-washed two-storey teak house (Wongburi's shape) grows under it. Teak and fretwork are the town's own (the slogan's
  "ไม้สัก", คุ้มวงศ์บุรี), the silhouette changes (plank → lace → house), and it returns at the payoff.
- **A2 ช้างลากซุงสัก.** A giant elephant drags a teak log; the front half already squared and golden, an adze peels the rest. A
  creature and it moves, but the "made by hand" part is small and the log reads as a plank at thumbnail size.
- Not offered: indigo cloth (Sakon Nakhon's cover), Cho Hae (a standing landmark), Phae Mueang Phi (no hand, no people).

## Map in one paragraph
The shell-shaped old town (~1.2 × 1.2 km, ~4 km round) stands on the east side of the Yom, which bends round its north and west
0.3-1.4 km out. Moat and earth wall are centuries old; the west and south stretches of the wall survive. The town grew east
(ถนนยันตรกิจโกศล, the airport 3 km SE, the bypass) and along ถนนช่อแฮ to the south-east. No railway: the line passes Den Chai
20.4 km SSW. Far places: Phra That Cho Hae 9.4 km SE on its low hill, Den Chai. Ban Thung Hong (mo hom) 4.5 km NE on the road
north. A high ridge walls the plain 6 km west; hills rise east and south-east behind Cho Hae.

## v1 build (cloud, 18 Nov)
`src/cities/phrae/`, 100 m/unit, origin the city pillar; Cho Hae and Den Chai drawn closer at the south corners (places.ts), the
west ridge drawn at the north-west edge so the finale has its mountain (grandeur). Score `scripts/soundtrack_phrae.py`.
- The cover's gable is the style frame's `Gingerbread` (shared code); the payoff's trestle uses it at hand size, and Wongburi itself
  is the same shape built (Wongburi stands from 2442).
- Den Chai is drawn as the line's end on the tile (the railhead in 2455; the real line runs on). Its bearing from Cho Hae is 263
  drawn against 235 real: both far places are squeezed into the tile's south strip (28° off, inside the 30° rule).
- ประตูชัย has no OSM node: drawn where ถนนเจริญเมือง leaves the south-east wall. The 2445 police post has no known site: drawn
  by the pillar.
