## Cover: A1, picked by the owner (14 Nov)
`kalasin_styleframes.jpg` (0/1/2/3 s), code `src/styleframes/kalasin.tsx` (`KalasinStyleA1`, `A2`, `B`). All three sit on
the whole land tile at legend scale, half made by hand at frame 0 and changing shape (experiments §17-§20).
- **A1 ผ้าไหมแพรวา (cloud's pick).** A giant Phu Thai loom; at frame 0 half the shawl is already woven, crimson with rows
  of khit diamonds; the beater bangs one row in at a time and the shuttle crosses until ~2 s; the shawl is cut off the loom
  and rolls out on the land. The province's own pride symbol (Ban Phon, Kham Muang; the royal visit of 2520), and it can
  come back at the payoff (the Phrae Wa and pong lang fair in front of the provincial hall).
- **A2 โปงลาง.** A giant pong lang hung at 45° from a post to a peg; half its logs tied on at frame 0, the rest drop in
  and are tied, then two mallets play it down the scale. The town's own instrument (the pong lang roundabout); gives the
  first sound.
- **B ไดโนเสาร์ภูกุ้มข้าว.** A Phuwiangosaurus skeleton half dug out of a red pit; the sand comes off, then it gets up.
  Korat and Khon Kaen already opened on dinosaurs, and the species was named from Khon Kaen's fossils.
- Why A1 over A2: the cloth's pattern is unmistakable at thumbnail size and it grows in a way you can see; the pong lang
  reads as a ladder until it is played. Unlike Sakon's blank white cloth (§18), the shawl is half woven and patterned at
  frame 0.

## Map in one paragraph
The old town stands on คลองปาว, a meandering stream ~20-40 m wide, ~180 m west of the city pillar; every founder site
(the pillar, ศาลเจ้าพ่อโสมพะมิตร, วัดเหนือ-วัดกลาง-วัดใต้โพธิ์ค้ำ) is in a row along it, and กุดน้ำกิน 0.8 km S (the first
pillar site, 2335) is an oxbow of it. The main ลำน้ำปาว runs ~5 km west. A pond ~1 km across (แก่งดอนกลาง) lies 2 km NE.
The town grew east to the new provincial hall (3.8 km E) and the university. No railway. Far places: the Lam Pao dam
19 km N, Phu Kum Khao 29 km N, the Thepsuda bridge 31 km N over the reservoir, Fa Daet Song Yang 13 km S, Ban Phon 48 km N.
