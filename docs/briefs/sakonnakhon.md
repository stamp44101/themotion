## Why Sakon Nakhon (owner, 4 Nov 2026: "สกล ตาก เอาไร" → cloud recommended Sakon, owner: "เอาเลย")

- **A symbol the town owns:** indigo (คราม). Sakon calls itself the indigo city; natural indigo cloth is the province cloth
  and a registered GI. §15-§17 say shares come from a symbol the locals would pass on, not a famous national one.
- **A cover that changes shape and fills the frame** (§17's rule): a giant undyed cloth turns indigo in the first 1.6 s,
  the white มัดหมี่ diamonds appear where the threads were tied, like Udon's jar being painted (skip 41.4%, share 4.7%).
- **A big, close event after the cover:** the Nong Han legend (a town that sank into the lake), told as legend.
- **A 0-to-100 spine:** Khmer prasat → Lan Xang stupa over it (Phra That Choeng Chum) → Nong Han Luang → Sakon Thawapi
  → Sakon Nakhon 2381 → Seri Thai in the Phu Phan hills → the town and the lake today → the wax castle procession.

## Covers (style frames, 4 Nov; `sakonnakhon_styleframes.jpg`)

Each measured against §17's rule at frame 0 (the symbol moves and fills ~1/3 of the frame; under the title box).
- **A1 (cloud's recommendation): the indigo cloth on the whole tile.** A ~50-unit undyed cloth hangs from a bamboo pole with
  its foot in a giant indigo pot; at frame 0 the blue is already a fifth of the way up and climbs to the pole by 1.6 s,
  leaving white มัดหมี่ diamonds; then the pole lifts, the cloth billows and a ring of indigo runs out over the ground.
  At frame 0 the cloth is ~35% of the frame's height, the pole sits just under the title's subtitle.
- **A2: the same, closer** (the cloth ~45% of the frame; the pot's foot near the frame's bottom; dyers at the pot are too
  small to read at this scale). Bigger symbol, less of the land.
- **B: the Nong Han legend.** A walled town with a stupa; the lake rises over it in the first 2.5 s and only the stupa's tip
  stays above water. Moves and fills the frame, but the symbol is a lake (it reads as "a flood"), not the town's own craft;
  better as the first event after the cover (3-9 s) than as the cover.

Why A1 over A2: it keeps the land under the giant (the owner's cover rule since Phetchabun) while still passing the size rule.
