## Why Chanthaburi (owner, 6 Nov 2026: cloud proposed Chanthaburi / Yasothon / Ang Thong, owner: "ลุย" on the first)

- **A symbol the town owns:** gems (พลอย). Chanthaburi calls itself the gem city, and its weekend gem market and its
  cutters are its own, not a national monument. §15-§17 say shares come from a symbol locals would pass on.
- **A cover that is half-made by hand at frame 0 and changes shape** (§18's lesson after the indigo cloth): a rough stone
  is cut on a spinning lap; at frame 0 half the crust is gone and blue facets show; the last chips fly off by 2.2 s and
  the finished gem lifts and turns. The change is of **shape** (rough lump → faceted gem), not only of colour.
- **A big, close event at 3-9 s:** ทุบหม้อข้าว, the night of 2310: the soldiers smash their cooking pots before the walls
  and take the town (§14's lesson: the first event after the cover must be big and close).
- **A 0-to-100 spine:** Mueang Phaniat (Khmer-era port) → the town on the Chanthaburi River → Taksin 2310 → the forts at
  the river mouth → the French hold the town 2436-2447 → the Catholic quarter and the cathedral → the waterfront →
  the gem market and the orchards today.

## Covers (style frames, 6 Nov; `chanthaburi_styleframes.jpg`)

Each measured against §17-§18 at frame 0: the symbol moves, fills ~1/3 of the frame, is half-made (no blank canvas) and
changes shape.
- **A1 (cloud's recommendation): the blue sapphire cut on the whole tile.** A ~56-unit stone stands on its point on a
  spinning lap; at frame 0 half its brown-grey crust is gone and the blue facets show; chips spray off the lap; each
  crust piece that falls leaves a glint; by 2.2 s it is clean, then it lifts off the lap and turns. The gem is ~28% of
  the frame's height at frame 0 and sits under the title box.
- **A2: the same cut on a yellow sapphire (บุษราคัม).** Warmer and reads as "treasure"; the blue is the stone people
  outside the town name first. The owner (or a resident) picks the colour.
- **B: ทุบหม้อข้าว.** A row of black cooking pots before an earth wall and gate is smashed one after another, then the
  soldiers move on the gate (drawn by day here; the clip has it at night, with torches). It moves and is close, but at
  thumbnail size it reads as "pots and a wall", not the town's own symbol; better as the first event (3-9 s).

**Owner picked A2 (6 Nov): the yellow sapphire (บุษราคัม)** (first said A1, then changed to A2 the same day). B's pot
smashing goes to the first event after the cover (3-9 s). Research to confirm: บุษราคัม is a Chanthaburi stone (Khao Ploi
Waen) and what the rough yellow stone really looks like.

What is placeholder in the frames: the lap and its stand are drawn, not modelled from a real cutter's bench; the pavilion
is deeper than a true brilliant so its point reads; there is no hand (the stone is "being cut" by the lap alone).
