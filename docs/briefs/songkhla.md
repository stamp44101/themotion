## Cover: style frames for the owner to pick (15 Nov)
`songkhla_styleframes.jpg` (0/1/2/3 s), code `src/styleframes/songkhla.tsx` (`SongkhlaStyleA1`, `A2`). Both sit on the whole
land tile from the real map (the spit between the Gulf and the lake, the Singora hills across the channel) at legend scale,
half made by hand at frame 0 and changing shape (experiments §17-§20).
- **A1 หนังตะลุง อ้ายเท่ง (cloud's pick).** A giant cowhide puppet of Ai Teng stands on its rod in a banana trunk on the spit;
  at frame 0 his top half is cut and perforated (light through the holes), his legs still inside the rough hide; a chisel and
  mallet cut him free by ~2 s, the scrap drops, and he comes alive: the jointed arm swings up and points, the body sways.
  Ai Teng was modelled on a real man of Ban Khu Khut, Sathing Phra (Nu Nui and Ai Samo on Songkhla men too): a resident's
  character every southerner names, so something to share (experiments §19-§20). Caution: "talung" is tied to Phatthalung,
  so the clip claims the character, not the art form's birthplace.
- **A2 นางเงือกทอง.** A giant mermaid on the rocks at Samila; at frame 0 the tail is modelled in clay and the upper body is
  half out of a rough lump; a spatula shapes it, the clay turns to gold from the rock up, then she combs her hair. The town's
  own symbol (unveiled 1 Apr 2509), and it can come back at the payoff on the same beach; but it is modern, and a figure
  at legend scale reads less at thumbnail size than the puppet's black silhouette.
- Left out: Singora's forts (not made by hand), Ko Yo cloth (a loom again, after Kalasin), the conch-on-a-tray seal.

## Map in one paragraph
The town is on a sand spit ~1.5-2 km wide running NNW: the Gulf of Thailand on the east (Samila), ทะเลสาบสงขลา on the west;
they meet through one channel ~0.7 km wide at the spit's tip. The three towns face each other across that channel: Singora
on and under เขาแดง (west, ~3 km WNW, its forts, wall, the Sultan's tomb and the Dutch graves), Laem Son on the west shore
(not in OSM; an estimate), and Bo Yang on the spit's lake shore (the pillar, the wall stretches, Hub Ho Hin, the museum).
Khao Tang Kuan is the one hill on the spit, between the old town and the beach. The old railway ends 0.6 km NNE of the pillar.
Ko Yo and the Tinsulanonda bridge's north span are in the south-west corner of a 96-unit tile (`x -56..40, z -50..46`);
Hat Yai Junction is 25 km SW, a far place. Both waters are one flat shape each, cut at the tile edges (places.ts).
