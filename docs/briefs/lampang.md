# City brief — Lampang / ลำปาง

Status: research pass 1 (2026-10-17), written in the cloud container. It reaches web
search but not Google Maps, OSM or any ONEP host (`www.onep.go.th` and `nced.onep.go.th`
both refuse), so the footprint and the pins are still open (§1, §5a, §7). Every date
carries its source and a confidence level. Nothing marked [unknown] is to be guessed.

## 0. What the Korat numbers say about the story

Korat v2's retention curve (Instagram, one day in): ~67% still watching at 3 s (skip 32.9%),
then **down to ~35% by 17 s**, then nearly flat to ~21% at 59 s. The loss is in seconds
3-17. In Korat those are dinosaurs, Ban Prasat, Sema and Phimai: archaeology, no people, the
camera drifting over forest. Once the town and the armies arrive (18 s+) people stay.

So for Lampang: **the first beat after the title has to have people and motion in it**, and
the legend gets 4-5 s, not 8. The hero stands on the tile at frame 0 (experiment 1).

## 1. Wall footprint

- **Source PDF:** [unknown]. The ONEP old-town record for Lampang exists (ลำปาง is one of the
  36 announced old towns; `www.onep.go.th/open-data-oldtown/` lists CSV and **shapefiles**
  for them), but neither ONEP host answers from the container. A shapefile would give the
  outline directly, with no grid reading.
- **What is known (secondary):**
  - The old town (เขลางค์นคร) grew on the **north bank of the Wang**; the modern city is
    mostly on the south bank (en.wikipedia "Lampang").
  - First-generation wall: **~600 rai (~0.96 km²), ~4,400 m round**, mostly earth reinforced
    with timber; gates include ประตูม้า, ประตูท่านาง/ประตูต้นผึ้ง and ประตูป่อง
    (lovethailand.org listing for ประตูม้า -- one heritage listing, not yet a second source).
    Shape described as **oval** (tourism source).
  - เวียงละกอน lies south-west of the older เขลางค์ (Thai academic paper, tci-thaijo
    husocetc 63658).
  - 600 rai with a 4,400 m perimeter fits an oval of roughly 1.6 x 0.75 km, i.e. about
    20 x 9 units at 80 m/unit. **Use only as a sanity check on the traced outline.**
