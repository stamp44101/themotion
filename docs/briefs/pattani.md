## Cover: A, picked by cloud on the owner's delegation (20 Nov)
`pattani_styleframes.jpg` (0/1/2/3 s), code `src/styleframes/pattani.tsx` (`PattaniStyleA`, `PattaniStyleB`). Both sit on the
whole land tile at legend scale, half made by hand at frame 0 and changing shape (experiments §17-§20).
- **A ปืนใหญ่พญาตานี (picked).** A giant clay mould (3.25 km at 125 m/unit) on the plain between the town and Kru Se; at frame
  0 half the clay is knocked off, the crucible still pours bronze into the mould's head, a mallet knocks the rest off toward
  the muzzle and the fresh bronze glows and cools. The province's seal since 2482, the largest gun ever cast in what is now
  Thailand, and its founder ties to the Lim Ko Niao festival that the payoff shows. It returns gilded on the same plain in the
  payoff (the callback, "พญาตานี บนตราประจำจังหวัด").
- **B เรือกอและ.** A giant kolae boat on the bay's shore, its stern half painted at frame 0, a brush paints to the bird-head bow
  and it slides into the bay. Identity of the whole coast (Narathiwat as much as Pattani) and not tied to the town's story.

## Map in one paragraph
The town stands on both banks of the Pattani river 3-4 km above its mouth on Pattani bay, a ~12 × 6 km bay closed by the
Laem Tachi spit. The city pillar and the provincial hall are on the west bank by the Dechanuchit bridge; the Chinese quarter
(Anoru, the Lim Ko Niao shrine) and the Central Mosque are on the east bank; the Chabang Tiga palace is 1.5 km south. Kru Se,
the queens' capital, is 5.6 km east on the bay's south shore: the brick mosque, Lim Ko Niao's tomb beside it. Everything fits
one tile at 125 m/unit at true scale: no far places. The sea is one flat shape cut at the tile edges (plus the open sea's
north-east corner beyond the spit). Flat land: paddy, coconut, shrimp ponds.

## v1 build (cloud, 20 Nov)
`src/cities/pattani/`, 125 m/unit, origin the city pillar; everything at true scale. Score `scripts/soundtrack_pattani.py`.
`npm run check`: 0 errors. Stills: `pattani_v1_contact.jpg`. Decisions made on the way:
- The Central Mosque is drawn 0.4 W and 1.3 S of its pin and at 1.7x so it reads on the grow shot (80 px) and ends the clip on
  something big; ถนนยะรัง is drawn east of its grounds.
- ทล.42 is drawn ~150 m south of Krue Se mosque (OSM runs at its gate); the casting pits, the old palace and the landing at
  Kru Se are estimates (not in OSM).
- The cover's cannon lies on the plain south-east of ทล.42, clear of every road; that plain is `openGround`, so it is still
  open land when the gun stands there again in the payoff.
- The curse of Krue Se is left out of the picture and the captions: Pattani's Muslims hold the mosque older than the legend.
- Fixed on the stills before passing them (owner delegated): the hauled gun was unreadable (drawn 3.2 units, the haul road
  and the ground east of the palace cleared, the camera brought round to the east); the port's ships stayed into the next
  beat; the new town was empty (the town's outline widened to the real two banks: 76 houses); the gilded gun read olive.
